Cookie Policy

We use a small number of cookies to make the store work.

Last updated 12 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that let our site remember your bag, wishlist and preferences.

Cookies we use

NamePurposeType
velour_cartStores the items in your bagEssential
velour_wishStores your saved wishlistEssential
velour_userKeeps you signed inEssential
velour_ordersStores your order history locallyEssential

This demonstration store keeps this information in your browser's local storage; no tracking cookies are set.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. Doing so will reset your bag and wishlist.
