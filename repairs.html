Repairs & Care

Built to be kept — and looked after.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Our ten-year repair promise

Every VELOUR garment is covered by free repairs for ten years from purchase. From a dropped hem to a replaced button, we'll fix it so you can keep wearing it.

Repairs cover manufacturing and normal-wear faults. Accidental damage can usually still be repaired for a small fee.

How to book a repair

  1. Email care@velour.example with your order reference and a photo.
  2. We'll send a prepaid label or arrange a pickup.
  3. Most repairs are completed within 10 working days.

Care guidance

  • Wash less often — air garments between wears.
  • Follow the care label; when in doubt, hand-wash cold.
  • Dry flat away from direct heat and sunlight.
  • Store knitwear folded, not hung, to keep its shape.
  • Use a fabric shaver gently on pilling rather than pulling.
