Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email, phone and password.
  • Order details — items, delivery address and payment method.
  • Usage data — pages viewed and products saved, to improve your experience.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, provide customer care, prevent fraud, and — with your consent — send marketing you can opt out of at any time.

Payments

Payments are handled by PCI-compliant providers. We never store full card numbers on our servers.

Sharing

We share data only with delivery partners and payment processors necessary to fulfil your order. We never sell your personal data.

Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, by emailing care@velour.example.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details on the cookies we use.
