Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about delivery, tracking and returns.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Delivery within Pakistan

We deliver nationwide through trusted couriers. Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day.

ServiceTimeCost
Standard delivery3–5 working daysRs 250
Express delivery1–2 working daysRs 600
Orders over Rs 5,0003–5 working daysFree
Free standard delivery applies automatically to all orders over Rs 5,000.

Order tracking

Once dispatched you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS. You can also view live status under My Account → Orders.

Returns

Changed your mind? You have 30 days from delivery to return any unworn item in its original packaging with tags attached.

  1. Open My Account and select the order.
  2. Choose the item(s) you wish to return and a reason.
  3. We'll arrange a free pickup within 2 working days.
  4. Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of inspection.

Exceptions

For hygiene reasons, fragrance, jewellery and made-to-order items cannot be returned unless faulty.

Exchanges

Sizes and colours can be exchanged free of charge, subject to availability.
